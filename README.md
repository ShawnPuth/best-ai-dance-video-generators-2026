## The No Scrubs AI dance trend, in short

In October 2026 a fan-made AI remake of the chorus scene from TLC's 1999 "No Scrubs" video — the chrome spaceship set, the lead in the middle, two dancers at her sides, the finger wag — spread across TikTok. People wanted the same shot with **their own face in the middle spot**, and tools appeared within days to do exactly that: upload a photo, get a 15-second vertical video with the chorus in it, download, post.

Because the Google and TikTok searches for this trend are mostly "how do I make it" and "what app does this", the tools below are compared on the things that actually differ: how many photos they need, whether the real chorus is in the exported video, length and format, price, and whether you can use them without the CapCut app.

## 4. General AI dance video generators

Not built for one trend — broader tools that can also dance your photo, each with a different trade-off.

- **[Viggle AI](https://viggle.ai)** — the best-known photo-to-dance tool: a big library of dance motions you apply to a character photo. Free tier to try; best when you want *a* dance, not one specific scene.
- **[Kling AI](https://kling.ai)** — its **Motion Control** mode copies the movement from a reference video onto your photo with strong motion fidelity. The pro route: you bring the choreography clip yourself. Credit-based.
- **CapCut (templates)** — the free route most TikTok posts start from, and the one with the most friction: the No Scrubs template lives inside the app, and depending on your region it can be **region-locked, queued, or gated behind app credits**. The dedicated maker above exists largely because of those three failure modes.
- **[freebeat](https://freebeat.ai)** — generates a dance video to a song you pick; good for music-first ideas rather than recreating a specific trend shot.
- **[Vidu](https://www.vidu.com)** — fast reference-to-video generation; a good sandbox for quick experiments before you commit to a paid render elsewhere.

## FAQ

For the trend as it appears on TikTok — one photo, vertical video, the real chorus — [NoScrubsAIDance (noscrubsaidance.org)](https://noscrubsaidance.org) is the most direct route: $9.90 per video, in the browser, no CapCut and no region lock. **How do I make the No Scrubs AI dance video?**
Pick a tool from the list, upload one clear front-facing photo (three if the tool asks for the full trio), pay per video, and download the MP4. On noscrubsaidance.org the steps are: upload your photo → the chrome stage, trio formation and chorus choreography are applied automatically → download your 15-second video with the chorus already in it.

**Can I make the No Scrubs video with my friends?**

**Do the videos come with the actual "No Scrubs" song?**
